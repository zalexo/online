Android Auto music player - Open source (MIT)

Four big buttons. Eyes on the road.

Drive Deck plays MP3 and FLAC files from a folder on your phone, or controls YouTube Music.

Build from Source View on GitHub

One screen, made for a glance

Drive Deck does not draw the car screen itself: it hands Android Auto the content, and Android Auto draws it. Drive Deck keeps that content short: what plays, where it comes from, and four big buttons.

Four large tiles

Previous, Play/Pause, Next and Folder, as large as Android Auto allows with four in a row. The tiles carry no text, so there is nothing to read. Play/Pause looks pressed in while music plays.

The track row

The song name, and under it the folder or playlist with the place in the queue: "Blues - Track 5 of 23". Tap the name to like the song; tap again to take the like back.

Favorites in one tap

The row under the song leads to the playing folder or playlist. Star favorites on the phone and it leads to them: one favorite starts with a single tap, several open a short list.

Liked songs

A liked song shows a heart before its name. The heart in the header opens the list of liked songs and plays it from the song you tap.

Your music folder, read where it is

Pick one folder on the phone once. Drive Deck plays the MP3 and FLAC files in it and in its subfolders, straight from where they are. It does not copy or upload your music.

The phone sets things up, the car plays

On the phone you pick the music folder, browse folders and playlists, and star your favorites. Playback itself is controlled from the car.

Two modes, the same four buttons

Switch between them from the header of the car screen. Exactly one mode is active at a time, and My files is the default.

My files Default YouTube Music
Who plays the sound Drive Deck's own player. The YouTube Music app on the phone. Drive Deck works as its remote.
Where the music comes from MP3 and FLAC files in the folder you picked. Playlists you add on the phone: in YouTube Music, tap Share on a playlist and pick Drive Deck.
The Folder tile opens Your folders and tracks. The playlists you added.
A tap on the song name Likes the song in Drive Deck. Likes the song in YouTube Music too. The heart marks the songs you picked in the car, not every song liked in YouTube Music.
What it needs A music folder, chosen once on the phone. YouTube Music installed, and notification access, which Android grants on its settings page.

Drive Deck stays on the phone

Drive Deck has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not even ask Android for internet access.

The car shows the screen. The phone does the rest.

Your music folder, Drive Deck and the YouTube Music app are all on the phone. Android Auto sends the screen to the car over USB or wirelessly, and passes your taps back. The car never reads your files.

Four permissions, none of them internet

This is the whole list the app declares in its manifest.

# uses-permission, AndroidManifest.xml
androidx.car.app.MEDIA_TEMPLATES
  car media screens
android.permission:
  FOREGROUND_SERVICE
  FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK
    playback in the background
  POST_NOTIFICATIONS
    the media notification

# not declared:
  INTERNET
  READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
  READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
  MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

One folder, nothing else

Android's folder picker gives Drive Deck read access to the one folder you choose, not to your whole storage. Pick a folder on the phone itself: a cloud storage app behind the picker could still go online to fetch files.

YouTube Music stays YouTube Music

In YouTube Music mode, Drive Deck is a remote. It reads what YouTube Music is playing and sends it play, pause, previous, next and like. It cannot see your library, and it downloads nothing. To read the player, it needs notification access, which you grant on Android's settings page.

What works today, and what Android Auto decides

Drive Deck is an early-stage project. It is tested on the Desktop Head Unit (DHU), the Android Auto emulator for a computer, and in one car.

Works today

Built

Your files and YouTube Music on one screen

The same four tiles play your MP3 and FLAC files or control YouTube Music. The header switches between the two.

Favorites

Up to six folders or playlists, starred on the phone. With one favorite, a single tap in the car starts it.

Liked songs

Tap the song name to like it, tap again to take the like back. The heart in the header plays the liked list.

One-tap like in YouTube Music mode Phone check open

A tap puts the song on the trip's list and likes it in YouTube Music, even if it was liked there before. Unit tests cover it; the check with YouTube Music on a phone is still to do.

Known problems and planned fixes are in the open issues register.

Android Auto decides

Host limits

Tile size and font

Android Auto sets how large the tiles are and which font the text uses. The text cannot be made larger.

A shared screen

On a wide car screen, Android Auto keeps a navigation panel next to the app. Only navigation apps open full screen.

No moving text

A long song name is cut with an ellipsis. Android Auto draws the text and does not scroll it.

Google Play only, in a real car

A real car lists only apps installed from Google Play. Drive Deck is there through internal testing only, with no public store page.

Try it on your computer

Android Auto shows a build you install yourself only on the DHU. That is the way to try Drive Deck today.

Before you start

  1. A phone with Android 8.0 or later and Android Auto.
  2. To build: Android Studio with its bundled JDK, and the Android SDK for API level 36.
  3. The DHU on your computer, with "Unknown sources" turned on in Android Auto's developer settings.
  4. In a car, Android Auto lists only apps from Google Play, and Drive Deck is in Google Play internal testing only for now.

Step by step: Testing on the DHU.

Build from source

Clone the repository and install the debug build on your phone.

shell 
$ git clone https://github.com/zalexo/media-app.git
$ cd media-app
$ ./gradlew installDebug

The debug build installs as Drive Deck (dev), so it can sit next to a build from Google Play.
